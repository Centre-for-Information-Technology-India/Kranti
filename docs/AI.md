# Kranti AI Policy

AI reduces friction while preserving human agency and factual integrity.

## Allowed
Classify user text, extract facts, translate, summarize, identify missing information, suggest likely authority categories, draft complaints from supplied/verified facts, detect likely duplicates, assist moderation triage, and explain procedures from approved sources.

## Required
Outputs must be reviewable, grounded in available data, conservative under uncertainty, clearly marked when unverified, and editable before external use.

## Prohibited
AI must not invent facts/laws/departments/officials/emails/reference numbers, determine guilt, fabricate or alter evidence, secretly contact authorities, autonomously publish sensitive reports, autonomously resolve cases, expose cross-user data, or reuse private case data for unrelated tasks.

## Sources
Authority recommendations should prefer official government sources and retain source metadata. If a reliable authority cannot be identified, say so rather than guessing.

## Data minimization
Send only the minimum required data to an AI provider. Never send secrets or unrelated private records.

## Failure mode
If AI fails, core reporting/action workflows should remain usable without AI wherever practical.
