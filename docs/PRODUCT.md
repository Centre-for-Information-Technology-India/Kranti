# Kranti Product Constitution

## Mission
Kranti is an open-source civic action platform operated by Centre for Information Technology India (CIT). Its purpose is to help ordinary citizens turn real-world civic problems into documented, actionable and trackable cases.

## Product promise
**Report. Understand. Act. Track. Resolve.**

Kranti should reduce the distance between a citizen experiencing a problem and the legitimate civic action available to them.

## Kranti is
- A civic reporting and action platform.
- A structured public record of citizen-reported civic problems.
- A guide to appropriate official authorities and processes.
- A tool for drafting and organizing lawful complaints.
- A community coordination layer around shared civic problems.
- Open-source civic technology.

## Kranti is not
- A political party, campaign organization, or electoral tool.
- A court or investigative authority.
- An emergency-response service.
- A replacement for official government grievance systems.
- A system that determines whether an allegation is true.
- A system that autonomously contacts authorities on a citizen's behalf.

## Core principles
1. Citizen agency: the citizen remains in control of external actions.
2. Evidence before amplification: claims must be separated from verified facts.
3. Action over engagement: optimize for legitimate civic action and outcomes, not likes.
4. Neutrality: do not endorse parties, candidates, political movements, or ideological positions.
5. Privacy by design: collect the minimum personal information necessary.
6. Safety by design: sensitive reports require stronger controls than ordinary civic issues.
7. Transparency: explain statuses, methodologies, moderation decisions, and limitations.
8. Open source: prefer portable, documented, self-hostable components.
9. Low operating cost: reuse the existing Next.js + Clerk + self-hosted Appwrite stack before adding infrastructure.
10. Incremental evolution: preserve the production system and improve it through small, reversible changes.

## North-star workflow
Report -> Structure -> Evidence -> Identify authority -> Prepare action -> Citizen reviews/sends -> Record reference -> Follow up -> Response -> Resolution -> Verification.

## North-star outcomes
Measure:
- Completed reports.
- Evidence-backed cases.
- Official actions initiated.
- Complaint/reference numbers recorded.
- Authority responses recorded.
- Follow-ups completed.
- Resolutions recorded.
- Resolutions independently/community verified.

Registered users and page views are secondary metrics.
