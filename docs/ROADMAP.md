# Kranti Development Roadmap

## Phase 0 — Production hardening (P0)
Audit Clerk/Appwrite identity mapping, Appwrite permissions, evidence access, public/private field separation, moderation lifecycle, rate limits, retries/errors, orphan cleanup, audit logs, backups/recovery, and production configuration.

**Exit:** documented security/data-flow review and critical findings fixed.

## Phase 1 — Civic Case foundation (P0)
Formal case lifecycle, timeline, structured actions, authority association, official complaint/reference record, follow-up, resolution, audit history, and compatibility with existing issue/report/petition/campaign data.

**Exit:** citizen can create and follow a case through an action and status update.

## Phase 2 — Civic Action Engine (P0)
Authority directory, official channels, complaint templates, AI-assisted drafting, review-before-send, copy/download/email/open-official-portal actions, reference-number capture.

**Exit:** problem -> legitimate official action is low friction.

## Phase 3 — Local Civic Network (P1)
Nearby cases, maps/lists, duplicate detection, joining cases, community evidence, notifications.

**Exit:** citizens converge on the same real-world problem rather than creating isolated duplicates.

## Phase 4 — Trust and resolution (P1)
Evidence verification states, official responses, citizen confirmation, resolution evidence, status methodology, impact dashboard.

**Exit:** a case cannot casually appear resolved without a documented reason.

## Phase 5 — Civic AI assistant (P1/P2)
Multilingual intake, authority recommendation, evidence checklist, document understanding, complaint drafting, translation, duplicate intelligence.

**Exit:** AI reduces friction without becoming the authority.

## Phase 6 — Open civic infrastructure (P2)
Public API, safe anonymized exports, self-hosting docs, instance configuration, modular authority datasets, contributor tooling.

## Explicitly defer
- speculative blockchain/on-chain storage;
- complex microservices;
- expensive search infrastructure;
- autonomous government communication;
- political campaign features;
- engagement gamification unrelated to civic outcomes.
